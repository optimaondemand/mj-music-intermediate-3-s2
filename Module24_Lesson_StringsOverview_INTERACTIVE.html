📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
What are the four string instruments of the orchestra, and who was Vivaldi?
The strings are one of the four families of the orchestra. This week you meet all four string instruments, learn to order them from highest to lowest, and meet a famous Baroque composer who loved the strings: Antonio Vivaldi.

🎻 PART 3 · VIVALDI AND "THE FOUR SEASONS"

One of the most famous composers who wrote for the strings was Antonio Vivaldi. He lived during the Baroque period and wrote a famous set of pieces called "The Four Seasons." Each piece paints one season of the year in sound, and the violin leads the way.

Who: Antonio Vivaldi, an Italian composer and violinist
Period: Baroque, 1678 A.D. to 1741 A.D.
Famous work: "The Four Seasons," four pieces for strings
The four seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter
Lead instrument: the violin

Watch and listen to part of Vivaldi's "The Four Seasons." Notice how the strings work together.

🤗 NEED HELP?
Not sure how to order the strings? Remember: violin, viola, cello, double bass, from smallest and highest to biggest and lowest. Use the four-card visual at the top. Still stuck? Message your teacher in your Canvas Inbox.
▶  Go Further: listen for each season
As you replay Vivaldi's "The Four Seasons," try to guess which season each part paints. Does the music sound bright and busy like Spring, or slow and cold like Winter? Write your guesses in your Music Journal.
