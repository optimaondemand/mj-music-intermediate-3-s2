🎺 PART 2 · THE TUBA

The tuba is the largest brass instrument and it plays the lowest notes. Like other brass, the player buzzes the lips into a mouthpiece to make sound. Because it is so big and so low, the tuba is the deep foundation of the brass section. Other instruments build their parts on top of it.

Tuba: deep and full; the lowest brass voice; it holds up the whole brass sound from underneath.

Watch and listen to how the tuba sounds and how it is played.

🎵 PART 3 · MAJOR AND MINOR BY EAR

Now train your ear. Major sounds feel bright and happy. Minor sounds feel darker and sadder. The notes are the same family of sounds, but the feeling changes. Many sad songs use minor, and many cheerful songs use major.

Watch and listen for the bright major feeling and the darker minor feeling.

🤗 NEED HELP? · MELODIC DICTATION PRACTICE
Melodic dictation means listening to a few notes and noticing how they move. There are three ways a short melody can move. Hum each pattern below to yourself and feel the direction.
↗
Up
Each note higher than the last (low, middle, high).
↘
Down
Each note lower than the last (high, middle, low).
→
Same
Every note the same pitch (no higher, no lower).

▶  Go Further: listen for major and minor in songs you know
Pick two songs you like, one that feels bright and cheerful and one that feels sad or serious. Listen to a few seconds of each and decide which one sounds major and which sounds minor. Then hum the first three notes of each and notice whether they move up, down, or stay the same.
