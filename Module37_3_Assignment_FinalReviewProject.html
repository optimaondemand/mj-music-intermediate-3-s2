How do you show everything you learned this year?
The nine sub-pages
37.1
String Instruments
Name violin, viola, cello, and double bass. Give one key fact each. Name a composer or work for strings.
37.2
Woodwind Instruments
Describe flute, oboe, clarinet, and bassoon. Give one distinguishing feature for each.
37.3
Brass Instruments
Identify trumpet, French horn, trombone, and tuba. Explain how accidentals (sharps, flats, naturals) work.
37.4
Percussion Instruments
Explain pitched versus unpitched. Name at least four percussion instruments and sort them correctly.
37.5
Notation and Accidentals
Show a sharp, a flat, and a natural. Explain each. Label a C-Major scale.
37.6
Rhythm and Note Values
Show the note values (whole, half, quarter, eighth). Notate or perform a short rhythm in 4/4 time.
37.7
Treble Clef Reading
Name notes on the full treble staff, including ledger lines. Submit a short annotated reading example.
37.8
Music History and Composers
Profile at least three composers from this year (such as Mozart, Beethoven, Louis Armstrong, Dave Brubeck). Include each one's era and a signature work.
37.9
Personal Reflection
Reflect on your growth as a musician in Grade 5: your most challenging concept and what you are most proud of. Name one concert-venue job that interested you (performer, conductor, sound engineer, stage crew, or venue and box office staff), and say in one or two sentences why live performances matter to musicians and venues. Write at least one full paragraph.
What to do
1
Work on one sub-page at a time. Use your Music Journal. Where it fits, add a short recording (sing, clap, or read a few notes).
2
Build all nine in one Word document in your Music Journal folder, or type them in the Canvas text box. Attach any recordings or images. Use song and work titles only, with no lyrics.
3
Submit your work.
