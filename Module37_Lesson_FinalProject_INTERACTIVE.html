🎭 MUSIC AS A CAREER

Before you review the year, take a step back. A live concert or show needs far more than the people you see on stage. Here are just a few of the jobs that make a live performance possible.

🎤
Performer
Plays or sings the music
🎼
Conductor
Leads the group and keeps everyone together
🎧
Sound Engineer
Makes sure the audience can hear clearly
🛠️
Stage Crew
Sets up equipment and keeps the show running
🎫
Venue & Box Office Staff
Runs the building and sells tickets

Live performances matter for more than one reason. For the artist, a live show builds an audience, earns income, and lets them connect with fans in a way a recording cannot. For the venue, ticket sales support the building, the staff, and future shows. And for the audience, being in the room for a performance that only happens once is an experience a recording can never fully replace.

1. Which group lists the string instruments?

🤗 NEED HELP?
A nine-part project can feel big. Do one sub-page at a time, and start with the one you feel best about. Use your Music Journal to refresh your memory. If you get stuck, message your teacher in your Canvas Inbox.
▶  Go Further: make your project shine
Add a short recording where it fits (sing a scale, clap a rhythm, or read a few notes), include a clear example on each sub-page, and use real music vocabulary. Specific examples and your own voice are what make a project excellent.
📝 ADD A WEEKLY ENTRY TO YOUR MUSIC JOURNAL
Open your Music Journal organizer and add your final Weekly Entry of the year. For What I learned, list the four instrument families and one concert-venue job that interested you. For your reflection line, name one thing you are proud of learning this year. You will use this in sub-page 37.9.
