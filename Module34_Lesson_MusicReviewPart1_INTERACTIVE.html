🥁 YOUR RHYTHM AND NOTATION RECAP

Here are the four big rhythm and notation ideas about rhythm and notation, all on one strip. Read each card and say it out loud in your own words.

💓
Beat
The steady pulse you can tap, like a clock or a heartbeat. The beat is even and never changes its speed inside a steady piece.
🎵
Note values
How long a note lasts. A whole note holds for four beats, a half note for two, and a quarter note for one.
🔢
Time signature
The two numbers at the start of the music. The top number tells how many beats are in each measure, like the 4 in four-four time.
🎼
Treble staff
Five lines and four spaces where we read pitch. The lines spell E G B D F and the spaces spell F A C E from bottom to top.

📚 PART 3 · THE BIG IDEAS, IN SHORT

Here is a quick refresher on each idea before you try the review check.

💓 Beat versus rhythm
The beat is the steady pulse you can tap and it stays even. The rhythm is the pattern of long and short sounds that rides on top of the beat and keeps changing.
🎵 Note values
A whole note lasts four beats, a half note lasts two beats, and a quarter note lasts one beat. The shorter the value, the faster the notes go by.
🔢 Time signature
The top number of a time signature tells you how many beats are in each measure. In four-four time there are four beats in every measure.
🎼 Treble staff
On the treble staff the space notes spell F A C E from bottom to top, and the line notes spell E G B D F. We read pitch by where the note sits on these lines and spaces.
